# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in Pin It to be a respectful, harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level,
or perspective.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Assume good intent while addressing the actual impact of words and actions.
- Welcome questions and help people contribute effectively.
- Keep criticism focused on the work, not the person.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's private information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, sustained
disruption, personal attacks, and publishing private information without
permission are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may
temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior is harmful to
the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through
[their GitHub profile](https://github.com/johnnyzhoujz). Reports will be
reviewed as privately and promptly as practical.
