How it works

One hotkey in.
One question out.

Text, tables, and screenshots go to your board with a single press. They come back through Claude, or with a plain old copy. Scroll through the loop.

01 · TEXT

Keep the good part.

Answers run long, and the paragraph you'll need word for word is buried in the middle. Select it, press ⌘⇧I, and keep moving. The rest can scroll away.

02 · TABLES

Tables stay tables.

Pin a table and it stays a table. Headers, rows, and alignment arrive intact, ready to use the day you need them.

03 · IMAGES

Pixels too.

Screenshots and generated images are pinned at full resolution. The picture itself lives on your board, ready to send back into any conversation.

04 · THE BOARD

Everything on one board.

Every pin lands on a single board you can search, reorder, and retitle. When it's time to build, your raw material is already gathered.

05 · BUILD

Then build with it.

Ask Claude for the pin you need and it arrives character for character. Prefer to do it yourself? Copy any pin straight off the board.

The details

Small on purpose.

Pin It does one job carefully and leaves the rest of your setup alone.

MCP built in

Pin It runs its own MCP server. Claude, Cursor, and any other MCP client can list, search, and pull your pins mid conversation. The connection is read only, so your AI can quote the board but can never edit it.

list_pinssearch_pinsget_packetget_pin_image

You choose every pin

Pin It records nothing on its own. It keeps what you select the moment you press the hotkey, and it sits idle the rest of the time.

Lives on your Mac

Your board is a folder on your machine. There is no account to create, and nothing syncs anywhere unless you send it yourself.

Images keep their pixels

Screenshots stay attached to their pins, and get_pin_image hands the original file back to your AI on request.

Free for individuals

The capture, the board, and the MCP server are free for individual use. That's the whole pitch.
